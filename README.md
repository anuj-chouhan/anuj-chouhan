## About

I'm a Unity developer building games and interactive experiences for mobile, PC, WebGL and AR/VR/MR, working primarily in C#. I focus on gameplay programming, AI, UI, networking and optimization.

I can take a project from first prototype to final release on my own, or join a team and own a feature, working with designers, 3D artists, and voice and AI engineers. A background in animation, VFX and 3D art means I also contribute to assets, audio and visual presentation, not just code.

## What I Work On

- **Gameplay systems:** gameplay programming, enemy AI, UI, networking and game architecture in C#.
- **XR:** VR and mixed reality experiences for Meta Quest, hand and body tracking, and WebAR content delivery.
- **Performance:** profiling, draw-call reduction, lighting and mesh optimization, and testing on target devices, including large VR environments on standalone hardware.
- **End-to-end delivery:** from concept to release, including a solo mobile game published on Google Play.
